<h1>Combat</h1>
<h6>version: 1.0: Restructure</h6>
<h6>Documentation automatically generated from docstrings.</h6>

Attack rolls and combat instances.


### `attack`

**Usage:** `/combat attack <title> <character> <weapon> <enemy_ac> <hitchance_modifier> <damage_modifier> <advantage>`

> Rolls an attack with one of your characters and a weapon against the enemy's armor class.

---

### `start`

**Usage:** `/combat start`

> Starts a combat instance in this channel; only one can be active per channel.

---

### `reload`

**Usage:** `/combat reload`

> Reloads armor and weapons from the database.

**Permissions:**
- `Developer`
