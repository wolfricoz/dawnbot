<h1>Armor</h1>
<h6>version: 1.0: Restructure</h6>
<h6>Documentation automatically generated from docstrings.</h6>

Armor sets that characters wear in combat.


### `create`

**Usage:** `/armor create <name> <hp> <ac> <hitchance> <modifier>`

> Creates an armor set.

**Permissions:**
- `Manage Server`

---

### `delete`

**Usage:** `/armor delete <name>`

> Deletes an armor set.

**Permissions:**
- `Manage Server`

---

### `list`

**Usage:** `/armor list`

> Lists all armor sets.

---

### `edit`

**Usage:** `/armor edit <name> <hp> <ac> <hitchance> <modifier>`

> Edits an armor set; options you leave out keep their value.

**Permissions:**
- `Manage Server`
