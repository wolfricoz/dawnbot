<h1>Experience</h1>
<h6>version: 1.0: Restructure</h6>
<h6>Documentation automatically generated from docstrings.</h6>

XP commands. Members earn XP by posting in the XP channels set with /config channel and /config forum.


### `recount`

**Usage:** `/recount`

> Resets everyone's XP and currency in this server and counts every XP channel again.

**Permissions:**
- `Developer`

---

### `checkxp`

**Usage:** `/checkxp`

> Shows your XP and how much you need for the next level role.

---

### `setxp`

**Usage:** `/setxp <member> <xp>`

> Sets a member's XP.

**Permissions:**
- `Manage Server`

---

### `addxp`

**Usage:** `/addxp <member> <xp>`

> Adds XP to a member.

**Permissions:**
- `Manage Server`

---

### `removexp`

**Usage:** `/removexp <member> <xp>`

> Removes XP from a member.

**Permissions:**
- `Manage Server`
